Black Spirit Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Black Spirit Hub is an unofficial desktop companion for Black Desert Online. This privacy policy explains how the application handles information when you use its Microsoft Store edition or desktop release.

Information stored on your device

Black Spirit Hub stores information locally on your device so its features can work. This may include preferences, tool settings, planner and checklist data, saved market items, cached public game data, and optional backups created by user-initiated customization tools. The Microsoft Store edition stores its app data in its Windows app-data location.

Some features use files and folders that you select, such as UI layout files, portrait files, font files, and recipe screenshots. The app reads or changes those files only for the feature you initiate. Changes require confirmation when applicable, and the app can create local backups before changes are applied.

Online features and third parties

When you request an online feature, Black Spirit Hub connects to public or third-party Black Desert Online data and reference services to retrieve the requested information. Like other web requests, those services may receive technical information needed to serve the request, such as your IP address, device or browser request information, and the requested URL. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Some app views can display third-party web content. Those sites may use their own cookies or local web cache according to their own policies.

What Black Spirit Hub does not operate

Black Spirit Hub does not require an account or sign-in. Its developer does not operate advertising, a developer-run analytics service, or a developer-run telemetry service for the app.

Your choices

You control whether to use optional online and local-file features. You can remove locally stored app data and optional backups from your device using Windows or the relevant folders you selected. Uninstalling an app may not remove all locally stored content, depending on your Windows settings.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or concerns, open an issue at github.com/Chucksterboy/black-spirit-hub/issues.

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change.
